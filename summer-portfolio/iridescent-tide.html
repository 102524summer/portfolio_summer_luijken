01Context

A research assignment with a simple, slightly dangerous question: are interfaces designed by AI better than interfaces designed by people? I answered it by building both a brand and a way to measure it.

The brand is Iridescent Tide: dark and coastal, navy water with dusty lilac and dusty rose light on top, pearl white for the foam.

palette first, everything else followed

the brief

02Research

The research is the point of this project. Two versions of the same interface, one from AI and one from me, compared head to head.

starting questions

  • Can people tell which UI was made by AI?
  • Which one do they prefer?
  • What does "designed by a human" look like?
  • How do I make the test fair?

05Build

A working voting web app in PHP and MySQL lets people choose between the two interfaces, and the choices are stored for analysis. The brand and the interactive prototype were designed in Figma first.
