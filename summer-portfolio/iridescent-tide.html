01Context

Iridescent Tide is my personal styleguide. It is a mix of two memories: the beaches on holiday in Greece, where I saw the colours of a coral reef for the first time, and starry nights. Put together, those two are where the aesthetic comes from. Over it sits one line, stay creative, because I have been creative my whole life and I need to be able to show my ideas the way I want to.

The styleguide covers a moodboard, a logo of three cats, a palette with the reason behind every colour, handwritten fonts, and how I like to communicate.

coral reef + starry night

the brief

02Research

This one began with two memories: the Greek beaches and the coral reef colours I saw there, and starry nights. I collected images that felt like both, the ocean, glowing water and purple light, and made the moodboard first. The palette was pulled out of it.

starting questions

  • What do I actually like looking at?
  • What do a coral reef and a starry night look like together?
  • Which fonts feel like me?
  • How do I want to communicate?
