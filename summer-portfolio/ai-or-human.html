01Context

In my second year the brief was open: pick a software-related topic, research it, and build a website that supports the research. My topic: do people enjoy AI design or human design more, and which one gets more clicks?

The project came in four parts: a brainstorm, an infographic, a research document and the final website, called AI vs Mens, that puts the designs side by side.

the question: which one gets more clicks?

the brief

02Research

The research is the heart of this project. It started as a brainstorm, became an infographic, and ended up as a full research document.

The goals were to compare visual hierarchy, measure how people decide, and look at what they notice. I held interviews, built an application and collected votes. In real research I would add eye tracking.

It matters because AI is everywhere and almost everyone has used it to make a website. The question is whether people actually like the result. The audience is anyone who uses the internet, and people who work in software development.

starting questions

  • Where do users look first?
  • Which design makes people decide faster?
  • Do users notice the differences?
  • Do people actually like AI design?

03Build

The final website, AI vs Mens, shows ten pairs of web designs, one made by AI and one by a person. Visitors pick the one they like more, which takes about three minutes, and a results page shows the totals. It is built with PHP and MySQL. Votes are anonymous and stored in the database for the research.
