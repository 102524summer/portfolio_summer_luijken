01Context

An animation end assignment made with Three.js and GSAP: nine yellow stars that float and sway, react when your cursor touches them and pop when you click. A scene, a camera, two lights and something worth looking at.

The chrome coins and the armillary sphere on this site use the same toolkit.

everything chrome on this site is three.js

the brief

02Research

The research here is play. Try something, watch it move, delete half of it, try again.

starting questions

  • How do I make a flat shape feel solid?
  • What should happen when the cursor touches it?
  • What should happen when you click?
  • How do I keep nine moving things smooth?

03Try it

04Build

Each star is a flat five-point shape pushed out into 3D with a small bevel, lit by one directional and one ambient light. GSAP floats and sways every star on its own timing. A raycaster finds the star under the cursor: it grows with a bounce, spins once and cycles through colours. Click it and it spins away, shrinks to nothing and comes back.

  1. P-01Geometryextruded star shape with a bevel

05Result

A small interactive scene where every movement means something: floating says alive, hover says look at me, click says pop.
