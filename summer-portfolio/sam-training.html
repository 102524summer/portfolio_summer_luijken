01Context

Loop Robots builds SAM, a disinfection robot. During my internship I am designing a digital e-training for it: the place where people learn how to work with the machine.

An e-training lives or dies on small things. How much to show at once. When to let someone try. What happens when they get it wrong. That is the interesting part of the brief, and it is where I started.

the brief, short version: make learning SAM easy

the brief

02Research

Research starts with questions, not screens. These were the ones I wrote on the first page of the notebook, long before anything was drawn.

starting questions

  • Who is learning SAM, and when?
  • What do they need to know before they switch it on?
  • Where would a mistake matter most?
  • What should a good training feel like?
